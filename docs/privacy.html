Afterword Privacy Policy

Local-First Storage: Afterword stores saved articles, book excerpts, notes, embeddings, and briefings locally in your browser's IndexedDB. Afterword operates no backend servers and does not collect, sell, or transmit your reading history to any third party.

Optional Google Drive Sync & OCR: If you choose to connect Google Drive, Afterword requests only the narrow drive.file permission. Sync data (afterword-sync.json) and temporary OCR conversions exist exclusively inside your own Google Drive account and are never accessible to the developer.

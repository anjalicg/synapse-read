1. Download Pre-Built Extension

No Node.js or build tools required. Download the pre-compiled extension bundle and unzip it.

Download synapse-read.zip

2. Drag & Drop into Chrome

  1. Unzip synapse-read.zip so you have a synapse-read folder.
  2. Open a new tab and go to chrome://extensions (Chrome blocks direct links to internal settings pages).
  3. Toggle Developer mode ON in the top-right corner of chrome://extensions.
  4. Drag the unzipped synapse-read folder from Finder / Explorer and drop it anywhere onto the chrome://extensions tab.
Browser security prevents web pages from installing extensions directly—make sure you drop the unzipped folder onto your chrome://extensions tab, not here.

4. User Workflow: Using Every Feature

Step 1 One-Time Offline AI Setup
Pin Synapse-Read to your Chrome toolbar and click it to open the Side Panel. If you see the setup banner at the top, click Download AI once so Gemini Nano weights cache locally on your machine.
Step 2 Capture, Sync & Hydrate Articles
  • Save Content extracts clean article text from the active tab and automatically generates a local TL;DR digest + 384-dimension semantic vector in the background.
  • Save Link Only bookmarks the URL immediately without parsing the page.
  • Sync List imports links from Chrome's built-in Reading List (including items saved on mobile).
  • Fetch & Digest appears on link-only and Reading List cards—click it to fetch the article HTML in the background and generate its digest and embeddings in place.
Step 3 Explore Connections & Semantic Search
  • Click Related on any article card to reveal top conceptually similar articles across your full reading archive with similarity scores.
  • Use the Semantic Search bar to query by topic or concept across both saved articles and your briefing reflections. Click Open on any matching briefing note to jump directly to it.
Step 4 Generate Date-Range Briefings
Choose 7D, 14D, 30D, or a custom date window and click Generate Briefing. Gemini Nano synthesizes top takeaways with clickable citations, emerging themes, and a From Your Archive section linking older related reads. (In fallback mode, use Copy Prompt to paste into any LLM.)
Step 5 Listen & Write 1,000-Word Reflections
  • Click Listen inside any briefing card (and pick a natural system voice from the dropdown) for offline audio narration.
  • Type up to 1,000 words in My Notes & Reflections—your notes auto-save to IndexedDB and are automatically indexed for semantic search.
Step 6 Export Markdown & Backup / Restore Archive
  • Export .md (inside the briefing card) downloads a clean Markdown note with YAML frontmatter, the briefing summary, and your reflections—ready for Obsidian, Logseq, or Notion.
  • Backup (top header) exports your entire IndexedDB database—including all articles, digests, 384-dimension embeddings, briefings, and notes—as a single .

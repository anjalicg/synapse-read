branch // 02 — reading & synthesis workflow (hover any leaf to trace links)
leaf:01 · warmup one-time

Cache Offline Model Weights

Pin Synapse-Read to your toolbar and open the Side Panel. Click Download AI on the setup banner once so Gemini Nano caches locally on disk—every briefing after runs completely offline.

leaf:02 · ingest & annotate readability + notes

Capture, Sync & Annotate

  • Save Content extracts clean prose and auto-generates a TL;DR digest + 384-dim vector.
  • Sync List & Fetch & Digest import and hydrate Chrome Reading List links in the background.
  • + Note attaches searchable personal annotations to any saved post or bookmark.
leaf:03 · graph-recall 10,000+ post scale

Trace Related Synapses

  • Click Related on any card to surface conceptually similar reads—weighted by both digests and your attached post notes.
  • Query Semantic Search across posts, post notes, and briefing reflections, or click Open on matching notes.
leaf:04 · synthesize 7D / 14D / 30D

Generate Date-Range Briefings

Pick 7D, 14D, 30D, or a custom range and click Generate Briefing. Nano distills top takeaways with inline citations and surfaces a From Your Archive bridge to older related reads.

leaf:05 · voice & notes 1,000-word limit

Listen & Write Reflections

  • Click Listen and pick a natural system voice for offline audio narration.
  • Jot up to 1,000 words in My Notes & Reflections—notes auto-save and auto-index into your semantic search graph.
leaf:06 · portability .md + merge .json

Export Markdown & Merge Backups

  • Export .md exports the briefing, your reflections, and all saved posts with their attached notes for that date window.
  • Backup & Restore seamlessly merge .json archives—deduplicating URLs, keeping hydrated text, and combining distinct notes with ---.
